About me

A scientist interested in how materials behave.

This page offers a concise view of my background, the work I enjoy, and what you can expect to find across this website.

Who I am

I am Nagaarjhuna Arumugakani, a materials scientist based in Köln, Germany. My work brings together experimental materials research, electron microscopy and microanalysis, thin-film processing, electrochemistry, industrial quality control, and scientific data analysis.

I am interested in turning complex experimental observations into clear technical understanding. This means working carefully with materials, measurement methods, data, uncertainty, and the practical context in which a technical question needs to be solved.

This website is a place to share selected projects, technical notes, conference reflections, and parts of my professional journey. It is intended to be useful to collaborators, fellow researchers, employers, and anyone interested in practical materials science.

Background

Materials science and technical research, with experience across functional materials, microscopy, surface and thin-film investigation, electrochemistry, and industrial inspection.

Current focus

Electron microscopy, thin films, scientific analysis, materials characterisation, practical technical communication, and continuing professional development.

What I enjoy

Learning, scientific problem-solving, technical discussion, clear communication, travel by public transport, walking, and exploring ideas through writing.

What you will find here

Projects, technical workflows, notes on materials science and microscopy, conference reflections, selected work, and updates as this portfolio develops.
