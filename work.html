01 · Microstructure

Electron microscopy & microanalysis

Electron microscopy provides a direct route to questions about microstructure, interfaces, composition, and materials behaviour. My work includes SEM, TEM, STEM, and EDX/EDS-related workflows, with emphasis on connecting imaging and microanalysis to clear, reproducible interpretation.

Thin films & vacuum processing

Thin-film research brings processing, surfaces, interfaces, and functional properties together. I have experience in vacuum-based thin-film processing and in studying how material structure and interfaces influence the performance and behaviour of a system.

03 · Evidence

Characterisation & scientific data analysis

Reliable technical conclusions depend on both the measurement and the path from raw data to interpretation. I use materials-characterisation methods together with Python-based workflows to organise, analyse, visualise, and communicate experimental information clearly.

Industrial quality, corrosion & inspection

Materials questions are also practical questions: how to inspect, document, troubleshoot, and make technically sound decisions.
